# Reconcile: Architecture Spine vs Brief Addendum

## Verdict

Amendable, no AD needs reversing. The spine structurally mitigates four of the five rows in the brief's risk table (B402 access, Binance MCP, settlement window, scope order) but is silent on row 3 (faucet dry, RPC lag) and on three single points of failure its own choices created: the self-hosted facilitator, the LLM call inside the paid x402 timeout, and an ephemeral tunnel URL baked into on-chain `agentURI`. Two stack changes the pitch leans on (USDT to a team token, Agent OS MCP to plain REST) are not stated as changes. One Open Question contradicts a confirmed fact in the brief.

Severity: **H** breaks a demo beat or loses funds; **M** costs rehearsal time or a judge question; **L** wording.

## Unmitigated risks

Brief §10 rows and the operational risks behind them, checked against every AD, the structural seed, Deferred, and Open Questions.

- **R-1 (H) RPC lag turns a landed payment into `payment_failed`.** Brief row 3. Engine paid timeout is 15 s; the agent settles on-chain inside that window. If the receipt is slow, the engine resends the same header, the facilitator's verify sees a used EIP-3009 nonce, and the engine records `payment_failed` while tUSD already left the wallet. Fix: `agent-kit` caches the 200 response keyed by the authorization nonce for 5 minutes and replays it on a retry with the same `PAYMENT-SIGNATURE`; state this in AD-6 next to "retried at most once".
- **R-2 (H) One public RPC, no retry, no fallback.** Brief row 3. The spine names a single rate-limited public endpoint for the worker and the facilitator; PRD §7 promises three RPC retries without re-signing, and the spine does not carry it. Fix: `RPC_URLS` (comma-separated, 2 or 3 public testnet endpoints) consumed by viem `fallback([...http])` with `retryCount: 3` in `adapters/chain` and `apps/facilitator`; add to Conventions.
- **R-3 (H) Facilitator is a team-owned single point of failure with no health or nonce rule.** The brief's fallback became the primary path, so "facilitator downtime" is now ours. Every payment, every listing verification, and the demo's 10 x402 settlements go through one process, one relayer key, one nonce sequence; a stuck pending tx blocks all later settlements. Fix: compose `restart: unless-stopped`; `GET /health` on `:4020` returns relayer BNB balance and pending nonce count; relayer uses viem's `nonceManager`; a pre-demo check script (`pnpm doctor`) asserts health of facilitator, RPC, and relayer balance.
- **R-4 (M) Faucet limits and gas floors are unstated.** Brief row 3. The seed "funds gas from the platform faucet wallet" but nothing says how that wallet, the Platform Wallet (slash, reputation, verification), or the facilitator relayer are funded, or what minimum BNB each needs. The public BSC faucet drips a capped amount per address per day, so a dry wallet on demo day cannot be refilled quickly. Fix: structural seed lists the three gas-paying keys with a BNB floor each; `pnpm seed` refuses to run below the floor; fund them on day 1, not day 3. Note that Builder System Wallets need no BNB at all under EIP-3009 (the relayer pays), which simplifies the PRD's gas-floor check to relayer and Platform Wallet only.
- **R-5 (M) LLM latency sits inside the 45 s run and the 15 s paid timeout.** Brief demo 0:30 to 1:20. The research node's Anthropic call runs between verify and settle, so a 10 s completion under load times out the paid request and fails Run 1 live. No AD bounds handler time. Fix: `adapters/llm` sets an 8 s request timeout and `max_tokens` about 200; `alpha-research` falls back to the rule-based 24h-trend signal with reason "LLM timeout, trend rule" so the paid Call still succeeds; put "handler budget 8 s" in AD-7.
- **R-6 (M) Key loss is unaddressed and "backups are unnecessary" reads as covering it.** Brief §8 mandates fresh keys; the spine says where keys live but not where they are backed up. Losing `MASTER_KEY`, the Platform key (only address allowed to `slash` and `setReputation`), the relayer key, or `deployments/97.json` means redeploying the registry and re-listing every agent. Fix: one line in the deployment envelope: those four items are stored in the team password manager before the day-1 deploy, and a second laptop can restore `.env` and run `docker compose up`.
- **R-7 (L) Spot Testnet balance reset.** Binance resets Spot Testnet periodically; a reset on demo day changes the Platform Exchange Account balance and voids open orders. Fix: `spot-executor` `/health` returns `getBalance()` so `pnpm doctor` catches it; document that `EXCHANGE_BASE_URL` on the executor container is the Demo Mode switch (a one-container restart, outside AD-10's no-restart rule, which is acceptable for an agent).
- **R-8 (L) Quick tunnel limits.** Cloudflare quick tunnels have no SLA, rotate the hostname on every start, and are not meant for sustained traffic. For the live demo on the laptop this is harmless; the consequence for on-chain URIs is D-4 below. Fix: if a public URL is required, use a named tunnel with a stable hostname on a free Cloudflare account, decided on day 1 with the Open Question.

Mitigated, no action: B402 not granted (AD-6 self-hosted, Deferred switch); Binance MCP not on Spot Testnet (AD-11 direct REST); one-hour rule in a three-minute demo (AD-9, AD-10, PRD addendum §6); scope creep (capability map mirrors the §7 priority order). The B402 daily payment cap question from brief §8 is moot while self-hosted; add "re-check the cap" to the Deferred B402 line.

## Demo hooks missing

Brief §9 beats that need something the spine does not provide.

- **D-1 (H) Listing in 30 seconds is three sequential chain receipts plus a paid call.** Beat 1:20 to 1:50, and Run 2 must start by 1:40 (PRD addendum §8). Stake lock, ERC-8004 `register`, `AgentDeskRegistry.list`, and the Platform Wallet's verification Call each await a receipt under the spine's rules, and `listing.verify` is a pg-boss job with its own poll delay. Fix: state the listing pipeline in the structural seed: verification Call runs in parallel with the chain writes; `sendTx` returns the hash immediately and receipts are awaited together; `register` and `list` are sent back-to-back from the Creator wallet; target 15 s of chain time.
- **D-2 (H) Stake asset and refund asset are not the same thing in the contract surface.** Beat 2:20 "slashes stake, and refunds the user" and beat 1:20 "lock stake". `list(...)` and `addStake` are `payable`, which means the stake is BNB, while the refund is the Call's locked price in tUSD and prices are quoted in USDT. AD-8 also keys `slash:{call_id}` and `refund:{call_id}` separately although `slash(listingId, callRef, amount, to)` already carries the refund destination. Fix: stake is tUSD; `list` and `addStake` take `stakeAmount` and pull it with `transferFrom` after an approve (or `receiveWithAuthorization` to save a tx); `slash` pays `to` in the same tx; drop the `refund:` intent key or reserve it for failure-after-payment.
- **D-3 (M) Flat-day risk on the 24h-trend rule.** Beat 2:20 depends on `change_24h_pct` being clearly non-zero and on the same sign at call time and 20 s later. Brief row 4's fallback (a mocked feed behind a demo flag) has no hook, and AD-9 forbids anything but the market-data port. Fix: nullable `platform_settings.demo_trend_override` read only by the demo-mode market-data adapter, set by the Operator page, or an explicit Open Question line: rehearsal checks `|change_24h_pct| >= 0.5` one hour before, else the recorded video is the beat.
- **D-4 (H) Ephemeral tunnel URL written into permanent on-chain `agentURI`.** AD-2 registers `agentURI = {PUBLIC_BASE_URL}/api/listings/{id}/agent.json`; a quick tunnel URL changes on restart, so every ERC-8004 identity registered during rehearsal points at a dead host. The schema page's `FACILITATOR_URL` has the same problem for a third-party agent. Fix: AD-2 requires `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` to be stable before any `register:` intent is sent; use a named tunnel or a static host for `agent.json`, or a `data:` URI carrying the JSON.
- **D-5 (M) "Second tab as a stranger dev" cannot share the browser session.** Beat 1:20 opens a second tab while beat 1:50 returns to tab one still logged in as Linh. iron-session cookies are per browser profile, so logging in as Minh in tab two logs Linh out. Fix: deployment envelope notes tab two is a private window or a second browser profile; rehearse it.
- **D-6 (M) Reputation ranking at the close needs real prior settlements.** Beat 2:20 "reputation drops below the original agent's" needs Alpha Research scored above zero before the demo. The seed creates accounts and wallets only; faking scored rows would violate AD-2 and AD-3. Fix: `pnpm seed --warm` executes three real Runs through the engine in demo mode and waits for settlement, run 15 minutes before the presentation.
- **D-7 (M) The endpoint Minh pastes must resolve from the worker container.** Beat 1:20. `localhost:4103` in the browser is not `localhost` inside the worker. Fix: seed prints the exact URL to paste (compose service name or `host.docker.internal`); `.env.example` documents it.
- **D-8 (M) Recorded backup contradicted by wording.** Brief row 3 and PRD §7 require a recorded demo; the spine says "Backups are unnecessary". Fix: reword to "Database backups are unnecessary" and add "the recorded demo is the fallback when chain, RPC, or LLM are down; nothing in the software replays a Run".
- **D-9 (L) Tx hashes on screen should be clickable.** Beat 0:30 and 2:20 show hashes; judges click. Fix: Convention `EXPLORER_URL=https://testnet.bscscan.com`; UI renders tx hashes and addresses as explorer links.
- **D-10 (L) A Telegram hiccup ends Run 1 in "failed at notify".** Beat 1:10. Under AD-6 a handler error is a 5xx, so an `api.telegram.org` timeout paints the happy-path run red. Fix: `telegram-notifier` retries the send twice inside its 8 s budget; if it still fails, the Call fails after payment as specified.

## Fact contradictions

Checked against brief §12 and the confirmed items in §8.

- **F-1 (M) "Hackathon identity unconfirmed" contradicts brief §8.** The brief confirms the Binance hackathon, Payment Workflows track, deadline 8 Sep 2026; only the extra Build the Era submission (closes 9 Sep) and the submission format are open. Fix: narrow the Open Question to "submission format (public URL, repo) and whether to also enter Build the Era".
- **F-2 (L) Addresses, versions, and URLs are unverifiable from §12.** The addendum records no contract addresses, package versions, or RPC URLs, so the ERC-8004 IdentityRegistry address `0x8004A818...`, the RPC endpoint, and every version pin cannot be contradicted or confirmed by it. Fix: confirm the IdentityRegistry address on 8004scan or the ERC-8004 deployments list on day 1, and let `forge script` write it into `deployments/97.json` instead of the inline value (the spine's own convention says addresses are never inline).
- **F-3 (L) `ERC20TransferAuthorization` in openzeppelin-contracts v5.7.0.** Not covered by §12. If the pinned tag lacks it, the whole payment rail slips a day. Fix: fold into the existing day-1 Open Question: "confirm the OZ tag ships EIP-3009; otherwise vendor Circle's `EIP3009.sol`".
- Consistent with §12, no action: `exact` scheme on a team EIP-3009 token (BSC USDT lacks EIP-3009, so permit2 is the only USDT path); BSC absent from x402.org and the CDP facilitator (hence self-hosted); B402 switch requires `permit2-exact` plus a Permit2 approval per wallet; x402 v2 header names match the PRD addendum §3.
